Common Questions

First Visit FAQ

What should my child wear to the trial class?
Comfortable athletic clothes such as shorts or sweatpants and a T-shirt. Martial arts is practiced barefoot on our sanitized padded mats.
Do parents need to stay during the class?
Yes, especially for the trial class! We want parents to watch how our coaches interact with their child and see the smile on their face.
Will my child get hurt or be hit?
Absolutely not. Beginner students never engage in hard contact. We emphasize non-contact drills, target kicking, and spatial control.
Are there long-term contracts required?
No! We offer flexible month-to-month memberships. We believe families stay because they love the community, not because of a contract.

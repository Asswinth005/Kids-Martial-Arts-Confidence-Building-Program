Inside Every Session

Anatomy of a 45-Minute Class

Minutes 00 – 10

Bowing In & Warmup

Traditional bow to flags and coaches, followed by joint mobility, jump drills, and dynamic stretches.

Minutes 10 – 25

Technique & Kata

Strikes, kicks, blocks, and traditional pattern forms practiced with precision and loud warrior shouts (Kiap!).

Minutes 25 – 37

Partner Drills & Defense

Safe pad hitting, distance management, anti-bullying verbal de-escalation, and controlled movement.

Minutes 37 – 45

Character Mat Chat

Sitting cross-legged for a 5-minute lesson on honesty, helping parents at home, and doing homework without complaining.

Special Programs & Weekend Events

Formal belt promotion test where students demonstrate required kata, kicks, and knowledge before earning their next colored belt.

June – August · 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM

Full-day holiday camps featuring martial arts, weapon safety (foam nunchaku), obstacle courses, and anti-bullying workshops.

Dojo Etiquette & Parent Guidelines

To preserve a peaceful, focused environment for all students.

Arrive 10 Mins Early

Allows your child to change, remove shoes, and mentally prepare for practice.

Clean Uniform & Belt

Uniforms must be washed between classes. Belts should never touch the floor.

Parent Viewing Area

Parents are welcome to watch from our quiet lounge. Free Wi-Fi available.

Flexible Makeup Classes

Missed a session? Attend any corresponding age group class that week.

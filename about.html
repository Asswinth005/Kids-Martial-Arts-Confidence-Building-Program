OUR STORY & MISSION

Where Kids Become Warriors

Since 2010, Little Warriors has been a second home for families — a place where confidence is built one belt at a time.

武
Est. 2010 · Austin, TX

Built by a family,
for families.

Little Warriors began in a small garage dojo with just seven students. Our founder — a third-generation martial artist and father of three — wanted a place where kids could learn discipline without fear, and strength without aggression.

Today, we've grown into a thriving community of over 200 families. But our mission remains exactly the same: to build confident, kind, and resilient kids — one class, one belt, one high-five at a time.

What we stand for

The Warrior Code

Five principles woven into every class, every belt, every interaction.

01

Respect

For instructors, peers, and self. Every class begins and ends with a bow.

02

Discipline

Consistency over intensity. Small daily habits that build lasting character.

03

Compassion

Strength is for lifting others. We celebrate every teammate's wins.

04

Focus

Calm minds, sharp bodies. Training attention is as important as training muscle.

05

Courage

Trying again after falling. Speaking up. Stepping forward. That's bravery.

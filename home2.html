The journey

Path to Black Belt

Each belt is a milestone. Your child builds skills, character, and confidence step by step.

01

White Belt

Focus, listening, and basic stances. Building the foundation of respect.

02

Yellow Belt

Coordination, teamwork, and beginner self-defense. Confidence starts to bloom.

03

Green Belt

Sparring basics, goal setting, and leadership within the dojo.

04

Junior Black Belt

Mastery of basics, mentoring younger students, and unwavering focus.

Methodology

More than
kicks & punches

Our curriculum integrates physical literacy, emotional regulation, and social skills — all through the lens of martial arts.

Focus & Attention Drills

Short, game-based exercises that improve concentration and impulse control — skills that carry over to the classroom.

Respect & Empathy Circle

Every class ends with reflection, compliments, and a handshake — building character alongside technique.

Tailored Development

Programs by Age Group

Kids develop at different speeds. Our age-segmented martial arts system targets the exact emotional, social, and physical needs of each stage.

Ages 4 – 6
30-Min Classes

Little Tigers

A playful, nurturing introduction to basic martial arts movements. We focus on listening, following rules, sharing, balance, and having fun.

Core Milestones:

  • Listening to coach on first whistle
  • Safe landing & rolling mechanics
  • Motor skills, hops & high-energy games

Focuses on self-discipline, bully defense, physical conditioning, and structured belt advancement that encourages healthy perseverance.

Path to Mastery

The Belt Progression System

Belts aren't given — they are earned. Every milestone gives your child tangible proof that patience, practice, and hard work lead to success.

White Belt

Beginning

Dojo rules, respect, basic stances & bows.

Yellow Belt

Month 2–4

Front kicks, low blocks & focus drills.

Green Belt

Month 5–8

Combinations, balance & voice commands.

Blue Belt

Month 9–14

Defense tactics & self-defense escapes.

Red Belt

Month 15–20

Speed, power control & leadership roles.

Black Belt

Year 2+

Unshakable confidence & dojo role model.

Our Philosophy

The Three Pillars of Training

Martial arts is more than kicking and punching. We develop the whole child across physical, mental, and emotional dimensions.

Mind & Focus

Students learn sustained concentration, impulse control, and how to eliminate distractions. Parents report dramatic improvements in classroom grades and homework habits.

Body & Fitness

Functional strength, flexibility, spatial awareness, and injury-preventing balance. A screen-free physical outlet that develops a lifelong love for fitness and health.

Spirit & Character

Courage to stand up against bullies, humility to help younger students, and the resilience to get back up after stumbling. Real confidence that lasts into adulthood.
